# Project Pitch: Sachidaanand Hospital Portal

## 30-Second Elevator Pitch
"I designed and developed the Sachidaanand Hospital Web Ecosystem, a comprehensive 21-page digital healthcare portal. The platform unifies critical patient touchpoints—from instantaneous emergency SOS dispatch and teleconsultation video rooms to physician scheduling, administrative bed admissions, online pharmacy ordering, and dynamic BMI health diagnostics—providing an accessible, high-reliability clinical experience."

---

## 2-Minute Technical Interview Pitch
"In healthcare web development, user experience can directly impact clinical outcomes. I engineered the Sachidaanand Hospital Portal to provide zero-friction accessibility across twenty-one semantic medical modules.

The architecture separates clinical concerns cleanly: administrative staff utilize `ADMINFORM.html` for structured patient intake validation and bed assignment; prospective patients browse specialist physicians in `docter.html` with real-time schedule filtering; and emergency callers have instant access to `panic_button.html` for single-tap ambulance alerts. For remote patient care, I developed `videoconsultation.html`, which models a WebRTC teleconsultation suite featuring media device controls and clinical notes. Additionally, self-service patient tools—such as the interactive BMI calculator in `bmi.js` and the pharmacy ordering system in `BUYING MEDICINE.html`—provide instant feedback using client-side mathematical algorithms and localized state.

Across all pages, I prioritized fast loading on low-bandwidth mobile devices, clean medical color coding (sterile blues, emergency reds, recovery greens), and interactive 3D card depth using `vanilla-tilt.js` to create a modern, empathetic digital hospital interface."
