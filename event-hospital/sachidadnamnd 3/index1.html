What is Proactive Healthcare?

Proactive healthcare is a paradigm shift from the traditional "break-fix" model of medicine. Instead of waiting for symptoms to appear and then reacting to an illness, the proactive approach focuses on prevention, early detection, and continuous, personalized wellness management. It leverages data, technology, and a deep understanding of your personal health profile to prevent illness before it ever takes hold.
